About me

Remote by nature. Full-stack by trade.

I started coding at 15, helping my dad with customer projects — gathering requirements, designing databases, writing code, then back to the client with a floppy disk full of deliverables. Pre-internet full-stack development, before anyone called it that.

Then came the dot-com boom, London, and I never looked back. I've worked across the stack ever since — from Classic ASP to ASP.NET, from early web apps to modern cloud deployments — enjoying the people and problems along the way.

I care a lot about how software gets built, not just what gets built. That means clean process, CI/CD, honest communication, and teams that look out for each other.

What I value:

  • Integrity and honesty in everything
  • Creativity — finding better ways to solve real problems
  • Collaboration over heroics
  • Sharing what I know and learning from others
  • The wellbeing of the people I work with
